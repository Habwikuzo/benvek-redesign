Why modern buildings need engineered air

Modern buildings are sealed to save energy. Without planned ventilation, stale carbon dioxide, moisture, VOCs and kitchen grease can stay indoors. Benvek's approach is to match the right system to the space.

Browse applications

  • Air quality: balanced fresh air with filtration
  • Quiet operation: low-noise fan profiles
  • Efficiency: heat recovery and heat pump technology
  • Design: concealed routing and slot diffusers
