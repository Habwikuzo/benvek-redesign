Fresh Air Ventilation (HRV/ERV)

Filtered outdoor air and energy-recovery ventilation for balanced indoor air quality, for homes and commercial spaces.

Request a proposal

The need

Sealed, energy-efficient buildings hold in carbon dioxide, moisture and odours. Opening windows lets in dust, noise and heat. Energy-recovery ventilation brings in filtered outdoor air while recovering part of the energy from the air leaving the building.

General explanation. Benvek to confirm wording.

Benefits

  • Balanced fresh air delivery with filtration
  • Reduced stale air and moisture build-up
  • Heat recovery to limit energy loss
  • Quiet fan profiles and concealed routing

Applications

  • Homes and villas
  • Offices and workplaces
  • Other commercial buildings
