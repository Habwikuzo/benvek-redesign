# Homepage wireframe (top to bottom)

```
[Logo]  Home Solutions Applications About Contact  [Start an enquiry]
+--------------------------------------------------+
| H1 headline + intro + [Explore] [Enquiry]  | art |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Complete solutions: 4 cards -> see all           |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Why engineered air: text | 4 ticks (+ claim note)|
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Featured: Fresh Air Ventilation -> detail page   |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| CTA band: Tell us about your project             |
+--------------------------------------------------+
| Footer: about | pages | direct desk + social      |
```

1. **Header** (sticky): navigation and always-visible enquiry button.
2. **Hero**: states what Benvek does; two actions: browse or enquire.
3. **Solutions**: verified categories as cards, linking onward.
4. **Why Benvek**: need-led explanation using only claims from benvek.com.
5. **Featured solution**: path to the full detail page.
6. **CTA band**: phone and enquiry.
7. **Footer**: verified contact details; address marked "To be confirmed".

**Mobile:** one column; the menu collapses into a "Menu" button; cards stack; hero art moves below the text; buttons go full width.
