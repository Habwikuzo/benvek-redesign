# Visual direction

- **Colour:** Benvek green `#2D4C3A` (taken from the current site's theme colour), dark green `#1E3528`, air teal `#1B6F69` for links, pale mint `#9FE0D6` on dark, tint `#EEF3F0`, text `#17211B`. Body text and white-on-green pass WCAG AA.
- **Typography:** system sans-serif stack (Segoe UI/Roboto/system-ui), so no external fonts load. Large, tight headings; 17px body; lines kept near 60 characters.
- **Imagery:** the aim is real, bright photos of installed systems, diffusers and plant rooms, shown in context. Placeholders are airflow line illustrations in white and mint on green. No other company's images.
- **Spacing and shapes:** 64px section spacing, 6px corners, thin borders, teal top edge on cards.
- **Buttons:** solid green primary; white and outline variants on dark bands.
- **Rationale:** calm and technical, tied to Benvek's existing green identity and the idea of moving air. Few effects; no animation beyond the mobile menu.
- **Mobile concepts:** collapsing menu, stacked single-column cards, hero art under text, full-width buttons, and large tap targets. Implemented in `assets/css/style.css` (breakpoint 820px).
